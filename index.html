IV. Publications / Selected Papers•Reviewed & in flight 004 / 008
Selected papers & preprints · Nº 04

Where the ideas became arguments.

把想法写成论证的地方。

i.

Critic Rule Induction: Improving Temporal Knowledge Graph Forecasting with Generator–Critic Language Models.

Yingsong Ning, et al.

An adversarial rule-induction framework where a generator LM proposes temporal rules and a critic LM verifies them, jointly refining inductive forecasts on TKG benchmarks.

ACL 2026 Accepted First author
ii.

DLTKG: Denoising Logic-based Temporal Knowledge Graph Reasoning.

et al., Yingsong Ning (co-author)

Diffusion-based denoising over historical evolution paths, producing cleaner logical traces for more reliable temporal reasoning.

EMNLP Accepted Co-author
iii.

Reasoning Along Temporal Paths: A Hybrid Transformer Architecture for Temporal Knowledge Graph Forecasting.

Yingsong Ning, et al.

A hybrid Transformer with multi-hop reasoning paths and an attention-mixer — +20% MRR on GDELT versus prior state of the art.

ACL · sub. Under review First author
iv.

The Control of Intelligent Building Robot Patrol Technology Based on Raspberry Pi.

Yingsong Ning, et al.

Kalman filtering and FAST-RCNN on a Raspberry Pi patrol robot, integrating perception, control, and dispatch into a single embedded system.

Journal Published First author
